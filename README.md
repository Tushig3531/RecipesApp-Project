**RecipesApp** is a user-friendly website that helps you find recipes based on the ingredients you already have in your fridge. Here's how it works:

  **Add Your Ingredients:**
Create your personal fridge by adding the items you have, like vegetables, meats, spices, and more.

  **Search for Recipes:**
Enter the ingredients you want to use, and RecipesApp will show you recipes that match what you have.

  **Get Recipe Details:**
Each recipe includes all the information you need, such as cooking instructions, ingredients list, and preparation time.

  **Manage Your Fridge:**
Easily update your fridge inventory as you use ingredients or add new ones, ensuring your recipe suggestions are always up-to-date.

  **Benefits**:
Save Time: Quickly find meals you can make without needing to shop for extra ingredients.
Reduce Food Waste: Use up what you have in your fridge before it goes bad.
Easy to Use: Simple and intuitive interface makes meal planning for free.
